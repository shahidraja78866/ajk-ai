Privacy Policy

AJK AI — AI Video Generator · Last updated 23 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and a securely hashed password, so you can log in and recover your account.
  • Your prompts and settings — the text you submit and the aspect ratio, duration, and style you choose. These are required to generate your video.
  • Generated videos and job metadata — the finished MP4, its status, timestamps, and credit charges, so your history works and refunds can be processed.
  • Basic technical logs — IP address, timestamps, and error records, kept for security, rate limiting, and abuse prevention.

How it is used

Your prompt is sent by our server to the text-to-video provider solely to render your video. We use your data to run the service, enforce credit and rate limits, moderate content, and fix errors. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your prompts or videos to train models.

Sharing

Data is shared only with the processors needed to run the service: the video generation provider (your prompt and settings), our hosting and database providers, and — if you buy credits — the payment processor. We may disclose data where the law requires it.

Advertising

The free version of AJK AI may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob, when advertising is enabled on the service. To provide and measure those ads, Google and its partners may use device identifiers and usage data, as described in Google's advertising and privacy policies. Paid plans are ad-free: while you hold an active subscription, no advertisements are shown to you.

Retention and deletion

Generated videos and prompts are kept while your account is active. You can ask for individual videos to be removed, and deleting your account removes your prompts, videos, and profile within 30 days, except records we must keep for fraud prevention or legal reasons.

Your choices

  • Download your videos at any time from the Studio or History.
  • Request a copy of your data, or its deletion, by contacting us from the email address on your account.
  • Report any generated content that concerns you — use the Report button in the Studio or on any History item. Reports are sent to the server and flag the generation for human review.

Children

AJK AI is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not offered to them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, we will note it here with a new date. Questions about privacy, or reports about content, can be raised any time through the Report buttons on this page.
