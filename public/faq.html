What is AJK AI?

AJK AI is an online AI video generator: write a prompt, pick a format and duration, and the service creates a video with AI models, including sound options and voice tools. It runs in your browser — nothing to install.

New accounts receive free signup credits. Each generation shows its exact credit cost before you confirm. Subscription plans grant credits per week or month; credit packs never expire. If a generation fails, its credits come back automatically.

How long does a video take?

Most videos are ready within a few minutes, depending on the model and queue. Your video appears in My Videos (History) when it is ready.
